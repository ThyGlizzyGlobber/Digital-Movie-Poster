Advanced — quality & grain

Posters are downloaded at full resolution and kept that way, but scaled to this width before the grain is applied. Match it to your screen's short edge or a little above — larger just costs processing time. After changing it, apply the grain below to re-process everything at the new size.

Turning this off skips grain processing entirely (not just a zero-strength pass) - noticeably faster on weaker hardware like a Pi Zero W.

Advanced — screen resolution

The physical signal sent to the screen - normally its true native resolution, so it looks sharp regardless of what the Pi renders at. Forcing the new mode needs sudo install.sh -y run once after Save (see CLAUDE.md).

The canvas posters are actually composited/grained at, then scaled by fbi to fill the HDMI output above - lower than the HDMI resolution is fine, and keeps processing fast on weaker hardware. Detect display fills in both fields above from the connected screen, capped to a safe render size if this Pi looks too weak to composite full-size - still needs Save below to apply.

Discovery

Active discovery source

Only one source pulls posters at a time — scheduled and manual syncs both check this, so the two can never fight over the rotation. Takes effect after Save. Daily sync time is set on the Schedule tab.

Automatic cleanup

Counts from when a poster was actually added to your rotation, not the movie's real release date — a trending-but-older title gets the full number of days like anything else. Applies to whichever source is active. TMDb has no "still in cinemas" flag, so its own now playing list is used as the next best thing either way: a title past the age limit but still showing in cinemas is kept anyway. Your own uploads are never removed by this.

TMDb automation

Movie categories

TV categories

The number beside each category is how many posters to take from it. A title appearing in two categories is only added once, so the rotation can end up smaller than the totals suggest.

Advanced — popularity filtering

Applies to everything except Coming soon. Filters out limited and arthouse cinema runs. Around 30–50 works well. Use 0 for no filter.

Kept separate because unreleased films score far lower — popularity tracks current attention, and a film nobody can watch yet has little of it however big it will be. Even the year's biggest titles sit in the low tens, so keep this around 8–15.

How far ahead "Coming soon" looks. TMDb's own upcoming list only spans a few weeks, which is why major releases months away never showed up.

Titles in more than one category are only added once. Region filtering applies to movies only — TMDb has no regional popularity for TV.

JustWatch automation

Pulls from justwatch.com's Popular movies list, filtered to titles released this year — recalculated on every sync, so it moves on to next year's releases on its own once the year turns over, no manual update needed. JustWatch supplies the ranking only: poster art and credits still come from TMDb, matched by title and release year.
